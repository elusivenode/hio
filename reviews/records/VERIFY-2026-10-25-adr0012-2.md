## Outcome and regression

**F-REVIEW-2026-10-25-adr0012-2: verified_closed as a proposed design correction.** Revision 2 used an uncorroborated success assertion. Revision 3 makes the retained byte/value checks replayable, requires post-window publisher time and store time under evidenced semantics, ties an eligible attempt to a pinned run, and states exactly which facts remain trusted adapter assertions. It does not claim cryptographic proof of a request. A compromised trusted adapter can still lie; requiring an authentication system would change the accepted trust model and is outside this finding. The S1b gate must confirm actual `Date`, `Age` and provider timestamp behaviour before any dataset may claim finality (`docs/decisions/HIO-ADR-0012-staged-s1b.md:282-283`).
