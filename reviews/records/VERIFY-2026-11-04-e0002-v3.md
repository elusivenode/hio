## Open findings

### F-VERIFY-2026-11-04-e0002-v3-1 — medium, open: a truncated named window counts a partial final month

`compare` passes `closed=True` whenever a window definition has a finite end,
without checking that the panel reaches that end. `describe` and `dependence`
then append the panel's last date as a month-end mark. With an invented panel
from 2007-12-31 through 2008-06-16, zero sleeve returns through May, and a
50% equity return from 2008-05-30 to June 16, `compare` marked `2008`
available and reported monthly equity volatility `0.7071067811865475` and
monthly equity covariance `0.04166666666666667`. The five complete monthly
equity returns are all zero; the sixth return, `0.5`, is June's incomplete
month. The reported window end was 2008-06-16, although the named 2008 window
ends at 2008-12-31. The same condition applies to any named window whose
nominal end is beyond the last common observation. Include the final mark only
when the observed span reaches the window's final complete month; otherwise
leave the month out or mark the window unavailable. Cover this with a
truncated-window regression case.

### F-VERIFY-2026-11-04-e0002-v3-2 — medium, open: report reconciliation can omit a failed comparison

`reconcile_reports` silently skips a window when either report marks it
unavailable. For an invented USD `q1_2020` window marked available and an AUD
`q1_2020` window marked unavailable, it returned `{}` instead of a failed or
explicitly untested result. It also initialises
`wealth_equal_where_spans_match` to `True` for `full`, `before_2020` and
`from_2020`, where it never compares wealth: invented `full` reports with
final wealth `0.9` and `1.5` returned all three reconciliation booleans
`True` when volatility and drawdown constraints matched. The field name
qualifies wealth by span, but the `True` value is still presented under
`reports_reconciled` as though a check passed. Record availability mismatches
as failures and distinguish skipped wealth checks from successful equality;
test both cases with invented dictionaries.
