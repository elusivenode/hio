## Material counterexample: identical pins, different eligible values

Q-001 §4 makes a result depend on quality decisions in force at Q. Q-002 §4 and
OBS-INV-018 require byte-identical recomputation from the result's pins, but prescribe
only `evidence_digest` over captured receipts, `resolution_digest` over conflict
resolutions, and `algorithm_revision`. None identifies quality events.

Independent reproduction in two fresh `LocalObjectStore`s:

1. In each, capture the identical synthetic `synthetic.rate` row for valid date
   `2026-01-12`, value `3.60`, `published_at=2026-01-12T00:00Z`, at
   `2026-01-12T01:00Z`; ingest in `SCHEDULED` mode.
2. Leave store A unchanged. In store B, the owner records a quality decision
   `QUARANTINED` for vintage 1, with `decided_at=2026-01-14T00:00Z`, after capture
   and before the query cutoff. A test recording clock earlier than that decision
   satisfies the no-backdating rule. Both stores are otherwise valid.
3. Query `as_captured_by_hio(synthetic.rate, T=2026-01-15T00:00Z)` in each.
   Store A returns `{2026-01-12: 3.60}`; store B returns `{}`. Yet both report
   `evidence_digest=2dd5e2568e21c9f42462a2f3536a719b00806baf3ec69497c7ca2eeeedba309e`,
   `resolution_digest=4f53cda18c2baa0c0354bb5f9a3ecbe5ed12ab4d8e11ba873c2f11161202b945`,
   and `algorithm_revision=hio-obs-select/2`.

The views differ under the same `(mode,T,Q)` and all three pins, without tampering or
out-of-order processing. The same gap applies to a quality event removed or altered
after publication: the receipt and resolution pins do not detect it. A release's
`inputs` currently carries those pins and a selected-vintage cut, but no complete
quality-event history pin. A selected cut can expose a changed current value; it
cannot verify which owner decision history produced it or prove a previously absent
value remained absent. Approved Q-002's exact definition of `evidence_digest` as a
receipt hash prevents silently redefining it in this verification.
