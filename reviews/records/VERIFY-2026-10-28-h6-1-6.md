It exited 0 and reported receipt complete while the unsupported closure remained on remote `main`. The receiver itself made no new `main` push on this path. The author's positive published-import test likewise accepts a hand-built import with only those three trailers. This is a false verification of an existing publication, separate from the local-history publication defect closed above.

The published-import path should reject an import that cannot be reconciled with §5.8's required metadata and the receipt cut-point history, or report it as unverified/incomplete for the owner rather than `received`. Add an adverse already-published probe with a poisoned parent and missing trailers; confirm it never reports a completed receipt. An outside push cannot be undone by the receiver, but it must not certify that history as a valid receipt.
