## Findings

All findings are **open** at the reviewed head. The counterexamples below use the submitted
functions, synthetic fixture rights, and disposable stores. They are independent of the author's
claimed 150 passing tests.

| ID | Severity | Finding and reproducible counterexample | Required closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F-REVIEW-2026-10-24-s1a-1-1** | **high** | **Consumer rights can be checked against an unrelated source.** `resolve_for_use` takes caller-supplied `source_ids` and passes them directly to the gate (`src/hio/publish/status.py:76–97`); the verified release core is not used to establish lineage. For the published synthetic AUD cash release, I supplied a gate containing only `synthetic.unrelated` with all permissions allowed and `source_ids=('synthetic.unrelated',)`. `resolve_for_use(..., consumer_class='hio_internal', required=EXPLORATORY)` returned the release while the gate had **no record for its actual `synthetic.rba` input**. `append_status(... APPROVED_FOR_CONSUMER ...)` has the same caller-supplied source argument (`:47–68`). This defeats the release standard §7 current-rights recheck and rights standard §3 every-input rule. | Derive and validate source lineage from immutable release inputs/receipt records (including upstream releases), then recheck every actual source at approval and each use. Add a wrong-source adversarial test. |
| **F-REVIEW-2026-10-24-s1a-1-2** | **high** | **Approval for one consumer class authorises another.** `resolve_for_use` checks only a status level and the requested class's rights; it never compares `current.consumer_class` (`src/hio/publish/status.py:88–97`). | **F-REVIEW-2026-10-24-s1a-1-3** | **high** | **Missing receipt does not block a vintage or release.** `VintageStore._load` checks sequence but never checks a vintage's `receipt_id` (`src/hio/marketdata/vintages.py:56–70`); `build_releases` reads those vintages (`src/hio/jobs/s1a.py:181–183`). ## Verdict and gates

**REQUEST CHANGES.** The reproducible rights and receipt-integrity defects defeat S1a's
foundation contracts even though the 150 tests and financial fixtures pass. Findings 1–3 and
5–6 require implementation corrections and a new fixed-head verification. Findings 4 and 7
need an owner/solution-architect temporal evidence decision before semantics are changed;
the author must then implement and test that decision. The owner should also decide items 1,
3, 7 and 9 above as policy where they affect S1a acceptance or later consumer use.

The separate operating-model §10 fresh-session test (S1-A16) has **not** been run by this
continuing conversation. S1a cannot be accepted on this review alone. Nothing here approves
S1a, authorises S1b, verifies real-source rights, contacts vendors, deploys infrastructure, or
changes `macsmsf`.
