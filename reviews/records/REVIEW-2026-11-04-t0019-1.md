## Findings

### F-REVIEW-2026-11-04-t0019-1-1 — medium, open: leap-day strike has no defined maturity-based coupon schedule

The proposed spec at `specs/factors/rates.us.cmt_10y.total_return.yaml:57-58` says the new bond matures at `strike + 120 months`, pays on the maturity's semi-annual schedule and starts dirty at par. `src/hio/library/cmt.py:84` instead creates every payment by adding six months to the **strike**. For a 2024-02-29 strike, `_add_months(strike, 120)` is 2034-02-28, whose backward semiannual dates include 2024-08-28; the implementation's first payment is 2024-08-29. On invented flat 4% yields, `cmt_period(2024-02-29, 2024-08-28)` reports `coupon_cash=0`, dirty `101.9889024280` and return `0.01988902428`; a maturity-based coupon schedule pays 2 on 28 August and places the bond ex-coupon then. The following day, the code pays the coupon instead. The strike-date par requirement also needs a specified first-stub convention if coupons are anchored to maturity. Resolve the schedule anchor and par/stub rule explicitly in the spec, implement that rule, and test leap-day and month-end coupon boundaries with independent expected dates, dirty prices and cash. The current tests use a 2 January strike only.

### F-REVIEW-2026-11-04-t0019-1-2 — medium, open: spec treats decomposition residual as a pricing-basis error measure

The spec at `specs/factors/rates.us.cmt_10y.total_return.yaml:53-56` says the flat-yield approximation error is second order and that its size is reported through the decomposition residual. But `src/hio/library/cmt.py:138-160` computes both the exact return and every decomposition component using the same interpolated par yield as a flat yield. The residual is therefore `flat-yield return − Taylor terms`; it contains no independent zero-curve or traded-bond price and cannot measure `zero-curve price − flat-yield price`. Even an arbitrarily small residual could coexist with material pricing-basis error. Curve slope produces a coupon-versus-aged-par-yield difference even when the curve does not move between dates. Correct the spec's claim and identify a separate sensitivity or reference for the pricing-basis uncertainty before treating the specification as sound enough for a factor build. The current residual test cannot detect this error.

### F-REVIEW-2026-11-04-t0019-1-3 — low, open: H2 decision pack omits raw-retention duration

The H2 pack's decision table at `docs/research/T-0002-h2-reading-pack.md:34-43` recommends allowing raw retention with citation but gives no duration for the owner's decision. The governing matrix legend defines *Retain raw* as `raw_retention` **with duration** (`docs/research/T-0002-owner-verification-matrix.md:49`), and its shared rule at lines 103-104 requires the owner to state the duration for all rows, proposing indefinite retention for audit and rebuild. Add that question or proposed duration to the H2 pack so an owner yes cannot be transcribed into a retention right without its temporal scope. The pack otherwise presents recommendations as draft, and no rights value changed.
