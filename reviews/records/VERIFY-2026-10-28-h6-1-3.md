## New finding: recovery loses the refused-index disclosure

### F-VERIFY-2026-10-28-h6-1-3-1 — medium, open

Draft §5.6 requires a refused `README` diff to be omitted wholly, the import commit to carry
`Index-Diff: refused (<reason>)`, and the receipt's OD to list the refused lines verbatim. A
partial receipt must not be presented as including the index. `step_import` reports the lines
and marks `ctx.index_refused`; `status_word` uses that flag to disclose `PARTIAL`. On a restart,
`step_main_state` detects the existing import and `flow` skips `step_import`. Neither the flag
nor `ctx.refused_lines` is restored from the import commit or the reviewer's diff.

In a disposable bare-origin fixture I used a VERIFY branch whose `README` diff changed an
existing finding state, with `--publish --without-index`. A local pre-receive hook refused the
first `main` push after the tag and import commit. The first attempt returned 1 and listed the
refused lines. After removing the hook, the second invocation returned 0 and published the
same import and merge. Its final summary said `STATUS: received` but contained neither
`PARTIAL` nor `refused lines, verbatim`. Remote `main` kept the original `README` byte for byte;
the import commit carried `Index-Diff: refused`. A further idempotent successful invocation
also omitted the disclosure. This is the summary the receiver could put in its receipt OD;
without the earlier failed attempt it does not meet the draft's reporting requirement.

On recovery, read and verify the existing import's `Index-Diff` trailer and reconstruct the
refused diff lines for the final summary, or persist an equivalent receipt report across
attempts. Add a restart test whose final successful output is used as the receipt summary.
