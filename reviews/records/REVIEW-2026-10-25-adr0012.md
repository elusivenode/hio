## Answers to the assigned questions

1. **Supersession accuracy:** The §2 table quotes the approved revision 3 S1b purpose, inclusions, exclusion, acceptance, entry gate, capture clock and research outputs accurately (`docs/decisions/HIO-ADR-0012-staged-s1b.md:45-62`; approved HIO-ADR-0010 blob, lines 40 and 46-66). Its formal supersession is limited to S10-B2 and S10-B5, and to S10-B8(ii)'s wording if the FX source changes. The S1a column and its later OD-0042 acceptance are left intact. The new §4.3 timing rules, however, are substantive operational rules and have the defects below; their presence does not alter the formal list of revision 3 provisions.
2. **No obligation deleted:** §5 lists S1-A1 through A16, all S10-B2 items and the G2 evidence (`docs/decisions/HIO-ADR-0012-staged-s1b.md:257-285`; `docs/implementation-plan.md:119-140,193-202`). S1-A10a-c remain owed or explicitly reported blocked. An index triangulation cannot substitute for an original triangulation. The §5 permission row covers the S1b-1 datasets, FX source and triangulation references. A blocked item cannot count as G2 completion without a later explicit owner supersession.
3. **Gates:** The S1b-1 gate carries S1a acceptance, dataset-by-operation verified rights, timing, calendars, AUD regime, infrastructure and owner authorisation; it adds the OD-0038 controls. S1b-2 requires S1b-1 authorisation and its own FX-source, rights, timing, calendar, spec and owner decisions (`docs/decisions/HIO-ADR-0012-staged-s1b.md:121-127`; approved HIO-ADR-0010 blob lines 48-52). The staged gates do not in themselves waive the revision 3 conditions. The timing evidence required at those gates must resolve findings 1-3.
4. **Return mathematics:** The proposed cash `total_return` and `replicable_with_basis` classifications and the RITS applicable-rate patch agree with RA §2 and §3.2 (`docs/standards/return-accounting-and-factor-construction.md:74-90,116-153`). The RBA TRI formula compounds the previous RITS day's rate into the index dated today; the NY Fed's worked SOFR Index example does the same. Thus §4.4's no-label-shift comparison is correct (`docs/decisions/HIO-ADR-0012-staged-s1b.md:184-187`; RBA Cash Rate Procedures Manual §18.1-18.2; NY Fed Additional Information, SOFR Index example).
5. **Observation and evidence:** `valid_time` remains a market-local date, `captured_at` a receipt fact, missed runs gaps, and Q-010 conflict suppression unchanged (`docs/decisions/HIO-ADR-0012-staged-s1b.md:139-166,233-244`; `docs/standards/observations-and-point-in-time.md:27-50,101-133`). The additional quarantine rules are fail-closed. Publication and revision timing, vintage basis, and final-release claims have findings 1-3. In particular, the proposal must not infer a vintage's first publication time from an approximate schedule or infer a captured final value from cutoff alone.
6. **Rights:** The matrix labels permissions as recommendations, and `registers/sources.yaml` remains unverified. `unknown` and `deny` both refuse operations, but the former records missing evidence and the latter an explicit prohibition (`docs/research/T-0002-owner-verification-matrix.md:37-69`; `docs/decisions/HIO-ADR-0012-staged-s1b.md:189-231`; `docs/standards/rights-and-licensing.md:14-17,47-54`). No permission is verified by this review.
7. **Boundary:** The proposal adds no `macsmsf` dependency and limits publication to `hio_internal` (`docs/decisions/HIO-ADR-0012-staged-s1b.md:55-56,91-97,121-127,287-300`). The assigned diff changes no implementation, spec or rights register file.

## Findings

All findings are **open** at the reviewed head. The publisher references below establish timing mechanics only; they are not a rights review.

| ID | Severity | Finding and failure scenario | Required closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F-REVIEW-2026-10-25-adr0012-1** | **high** | **A scheduled capture can be given a `knowable_at` earlier than this vintage's publication.** §4.3 sets P to before 09:20 Sydney for AONIA and about 08:00 ET for SOFR, then gives any first capture within grace `knowable_at=P` and `captured_on_schedule` (`docs/decisions/HIO-ADR-0012-staged-s1b.md:131-145`). The RBA procedures expressly allow a deferred publication, and F1 website availability is only *usually* by 09:20. The NY Fed describes SOFR Index publication *shortly after* approximately 08:00. For example, if RBA defers to 09:35 and HIO's first F1 capture is 09:45, a 09:20 timestamp says this captured value was publicly knowable at 09:20 without evidence. A 08:30 SOFR Index capture following an 08:20 actual publication has the same problem if P is 08:00. This defeats the vintage-specific evidence requirement in observations §3 (`docs/standards/observations-and-point-in-time.md:38-55`). RBA Cash Rate Procedures Manual §§9,18.2 and NY Fed Additional Information, publication section, are the publisher sources. | Distinguish a documented upper-bound schedule from observed publication evidence for each delivery channel; use a proven per-vintage timestamp when available, otherwise a conservative capture-time basis or another explicitly approved rule. Specify grace and deferred-publication handling before `as_known_by_market` can claim the value at P. |
| **F-REVIEW-2026-10-25-adr0012-2** | **high** | **A release can claim to be final before HIO has captured the final vintage.** §4.3 schedules the final capture after W but calls a release final whenever its `knowledge_cutoff` is after W (`docs/decisions/HIO-ADR-0012-staged-s1b.md:146-171`). Example: RBA revises AONIA at 15:50, W is 16:00, HIO's second capture is 16:30; a 16:10 build with `knowledge_cutoff=16:10` selects the 09:45 vintage under `as_captured_by_hio` yet meets the proposed final test. This is a stale final release even if all timestamps are recorded honestly. The same interval exists between NY Fed's approximate 14:30 revision and 15:00 capture. The release standard requires its content identity and recorded input cut (`docs/standards/datasets-releases-and-artefacts.md:41-52`); a cutoff alone does not establish that the final value was held. | Require verified post-window capture or publisher revision evidence for every input, with `captured_at <= knowledge_cutoff`, and handle failed, delayed and unchanged final captures explicitly. Define finality using both the window and receipt evidence. |
| **F-REVIEW-2026-10-25-adr0012-3** | **medium** | **The SOFR Index revision condition is narrower than the NY Fed's rule.** The §4.3 table groups SOFR and SOFR Index under a revision only when the rate moves more than 1 bp (`docs/decisions/HIO-ADR-0012-staged-s1b.md:133-136`). The NY Fed says the Index may also be revised at about 14:30 ET when an error is found in the Index calculation, even with no SOFR revision. An Index-only correction between captures would be misclassified or missed if the condition is used to decide whether to fetch, mark a new vintage or call an Index triangulation final. The NY Fed Additional Information, “Details on Publication and Revisions,” states the separate Index rule. | State separate SOFR and Index revision triggers, and require the post-window Index check even when SOFR's rate and `revisionIndicator` do not change. |
